# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Public repository presentation, English documentation and screenshots.
- GitHub CI, tagged-release packaging and contributor/issue templates.
- Catalogue regeneration pinned to an upstream revision.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-11-01

- English by default; Spanish available with `--lang es`.
- F2 switches language and persists the preference.
- Localized UI, solar-system names, phases, compass and validation messages.
- Search accepts English and Spanish names regardless of interface language.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-01

- Progressive star labels as the field of view narrows.
- Close-up catalogue designations and HIP identifiers.
- Collision-aware placement, terminal-size limits and zoom hysteresis.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-01

- Offline positions for seven planets, the Sun and the Moon.
- Topocentric lunar parallax, phases, illumination and observer distances.
- Day/twilight/night indicator.
- Full-sky mode, including below the horizon, with `H` or `--no-horizon`.
- Reference checks against NASA/JPL Horizons.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-01

- Initial terminal planetarium with 5,044 stars and 88 constellation figures.
- Observer location, live/paused time, keyboard navigation and mouse dragging.
- Search, selection, zoom, decorative terrain and Arch packaging.
